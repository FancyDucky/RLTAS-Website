RLTAS 1.0.2

**Balls tab**
- Up to 6 balls, each with its own settings.
- Ball types: normal, puck, cube, basketball, beach ball, egg, pizza puck, football, spooky cube, anniversary, haunted, shoe, strike, World Cup, Ekin and Dropshot balls. Press "Load all ball types" once in free play to load them.
- Per ball: size, gravity, bounciness, car hit power, its own top speed, curveball (with strength) and a magnet (nearest car or a goal).

**Multiplayer TAS**
- Up to 6 players.
- Start and max boost per player; boost now drains like a match.
- Player names stay with their player numbers when you switch players.
- Bots without Bot AI stay parked (also with Physics on and after the reset key).

**Mirror bots**
- Ceiling mirror axis, a mirror axis per bot, and an "army" mode that puts every bot on your side.
- "Disable demos" now works from the Mirror Bot tab.

**Other**
- Frame Editor: UNCAP CAR SPEED / UNCAP BALL SPEED.
- Physics tab: Max ball speed (with Infinite) and Max car speed.
- Right-click any slider to reset it.
- New HELP tab with a link to rltas.com/how-to-use.
- Get Tier opens the purchase page from anywhere (main menu, matches, free play).
- Licenses are now signed by the license server, and work offline for 7 days after the last check.
- Garage fixes (no more respawn loops, the picked car follows its player) and effect colors no longer change the supersonic trail.
